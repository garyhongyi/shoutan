一分钟学会吃子棋CAPTURE GO IN ONE MINUTE

① 黑白轮流，把子放在线的交叉点上。黑先。Take turns. Stones go on the crossings, not the squares. Black goes first.
② 一颗子旁边的空点叫“气”。中间的子有 4 口气。The empty crossings next to a stone are its “breaths” (liberties). A stone in the middle has four.
③ 气全被堵住，子就被“提”走了。连在一起的子共用气。Fill all its breaths and the stone is captured. Connected stones share their breaths.
④ 吃子棋：先吃到对方的子就赢。围棋：两人都虚手后，谁围的地方大谁赢。Capture Go: first capture wins. Full Go: when both pass, the larger area wins.
